### Flagship (named, cleared)

**1. Instant quotes from CAD files for a laser-cutting shop**
Client: Laserbend, US sheet-metal laser-cutting shop · laserbend.com · Nov 2025 – present (WordPress plugin)
Problem: Quotes were done by hand from customer CAD files. Every quote cost staff time and bad geometry could produce a wrong price. Brief: "it needs to function like OSHCut."
What I did: WordPress/WooCommerce plugin (React front end, PHP back end) that parses uploaded DXF, DWG, STEP and IGES files in the browser, detects bends and part size, prices instantly with volume discounts, 3-tier shipping, tax-exempt document upload, Valor PayTech payment creating WooCommerce orders, custom-quote handoff for ambiguous geometry, quotes in a custom table with bulk admin actions. Later a full visual rebuild to an approved dark mockup with 3D part view.
Result: Customers get a price at upload time. Live on laserbend.com. Delivered through an intermediary first, then the shop ordered directly.
Stack: WordPress, WooCommerce, PHP, React, custom DB table, deploy script.

**2. Shopify shipping rules from a two-carrier rate contract**
Client: Ruckaway, Australian rucking-gear store · ruckaway.com · Sep – Oct 2025 (Shopify)
Problem: Australia Post plus Direct Freight contract with rules Shopify's rate table can't express: cheaper carrier by weight and zone, parcel splitting over 22 kg to avoid a $116.70 surcharge, free shipping over $300 with remote-area exclusions.
What I did: Turned the contract into 191 test scenarios, built a Python calculator to validate them, configured the rate logic in ShipX so checkout picks the right carrier and price, checked every scenario against live checkout, handed over product-weight setup notes.
Result: Checkout picks the cheaper carrier automatically.
Quote: "brilliant job setting up a very complex set of shipping rules… pleasure to work with."
Stack: Shopify, ShipX, Python.

**3. Wholesale pricing, Danish translation and a migration that broke checkout**
Client: Tom Rossau, Danish designer-lighting brand · tomrossau.com · Feb – Aug 2026 (WooCommerce)
Problem: After a hosting migration the checkout threw a fatal error, Danish subpages returned 404, menus did not translate, old-domain URLs were baked into the database, and order emails had stopped. They also wanted a wholesale side for retailers.
What I did: Replaced 51,967 old-domain references in the database, traced the checkout fatal to a missing WPML String Translation dependency, found the email-customizer plugin blocking order emails, fixed a B2BKing/WPML sync gap with a small permanent mu-plugin, ran a 110-item bulk translation, managed the move from Kinsta to Hostinger. Set up B2BKing so approved retailers register, log in and see their own trade prices, with invoice or card checkout by group.
Result: 0 old-domain references, checkout errors to zero, 111 of 111 translation jobs complete, wholesale live.
Quote: "Super professional and got things done very quick… deep understanding of the specific work."
Stack: WordPress, WooCommerce, WPML, B2BKing, Worldline, mu-plugin, WP-CLI.

**4. Automatic pickup-point registration and a multi-carrier tracking page**
Client: Vape Society Supplies, Canadian/US store · vapesocietysupplies.com · 2024 – Mar 2026 (WooCommerce)
Problem: Age-restricted shipments to PUDO pickup points had to be registered with PUDO's API each time a label was created, first via ShipStation, later via Shippo, without duplicates. Customers shipped through several PACT-Act carriers had no single place to track.
What I did: "PUDO for WooCommerce" plugin (v6.2.1): pickup-point selection at checkout, listens for the label event, registers the shipment once with order-level locking, writes the result to the order, manual and test paths, HPOS-compatible. Plus a tracking page that validates the number and routes to GLS, USPS, UPS or WANHub.
Result: Fulfilment runs inside the normal order flow with no manual PUDO entry. Repeat client across 8 orders since 2024.
Stack: WordPress, WooCommerce, PUDO API, ShipStation, Shippo.
