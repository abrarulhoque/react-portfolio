# Abrar's portfolio

## Register

brand

## Audience and purpose

Store owners and operations teams who need custom WordPress, WooCommerce, and Shopify development. Help visitors recognize their problem, assess relevant work, and contact Abrar directly.

## Personality

Individual, capable, plain-spoken. A cinematic monochrome lunar setting inspired by Abrar's BreakFree app. Clear content remains immediately accessible without waiting for 3D.

## Principles

- Lead with the customer's problem and concrete delivered work.
- Keep client names pending approval anonymous. Omit unverified claims and internal commercial data.
- First person, concise, lowercase copy. No agency positioning or invented results.
- Use the supplied new_profile.png portrait.
- Native scrolling, keyboard access, reduced motion, visible focus, and a pause control. Target WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS marketing, terminal-first portfolios, neon glass panels, crowded space-game interfaces.
